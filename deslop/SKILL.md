# Deslop

Strip predictable AI patterns from writing. Make prose sound like a specific human wrote it.

## Core rules

1. Cut filler phrases and throat-clearing ("Here's the thing", "In this section we'll explore").
2. Break formulaic structures: binary contrasts ("Not X. Y."), dramatic fragments, self-posed rhetorical questions.
3. Kill tropes: "quietly", "delve", "serves as", false ranges, grandiose stakes, patronizing analogies.
4. Active voice with named actors. "The team fixed it" not "The complaint becomes a fix."
5. Be specific. If you cannot name the expert, you do not have a source.
6. Match register. No business buzzwords leaking into technical prose.
7. Vary rhythm. Two items beat three. No em dashes.
8. Trust readers. Skip "Let's break this down" and fractal summaries.
9. No bold-first bullets, unicode arrows, or "In conclusion".
10. One point per section. Do not restate the same argument ten ways.

## Pre-send

Heavy -ly adverbs? Cut. Passive? Name the actor. "Not X, it's Y"? State Y. Em dash? Comma or period. Same metaphor twice? Cut the repeat.

When reviewing, rate directness, rhythm, trust, authenticity, density. Below 35/50: revise.
